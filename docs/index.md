# Welcome to SQL Power Hour

Welcome to SQL Power Hour!

You were hired because you have the skills, knowledge, and potential to deliver great software. This program isn't here to question that or take you back to square one. Instead, it's an opportunity to sharpen your SQL skills, explore different approaches to solving problems, and build on the experience you already have.

## Why SQL Power Hour?

SQL is more than a skill we list on our CVs or use to answer interview questions. It's a tool we rely on to build, maintain, and support real-world applications. Working with client projects means encountering different database structures, complex queries, unfamiliar business requirements, and situations where getting the right result is only part of the solution.

SQL Power Hour gives us a dedicated space to practise, challenge ourselves, and learn from one another. Through a series of practical exercises built around a shared database, we'll explore everything from writing straightforward queries to solving more complex data problems.

You might discover a new way to approach a familiar problem, revisit a concept you haven't used in a while, or learn a technique that makes your queries more effective. And that's the point: there's always room to grow, regardless of how much you already know.

## What to Expect

Each session focuses on a particular SQL topic, with exercises of varying difficulty. You'll work through practical problems, apply your knowledge, and have opportunities to compare approaches and discuss solutions.

Some exercises will be straightforward. Others will require a little more thought. The goal isn't simply to complete as many questions as possible, but to understand the problems you're solving and the reasoning behind your solutions.

The Bigger Picture

At the end of the day, we're not here just to write queries that work. We're here to become more confident, adaptable, and effective developers who can work with data in different environments and contribute meaningfully to the projects we support.

Bring the knowledge you already have, be open to learning something new, and don't be afraid to tackle a challenging problem.
